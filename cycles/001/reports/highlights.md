# Research highlights and anomalies

Status: exploration; all scientific interpretation is exploratory. Research-phase observations below are retained with current experimental updates. Predictions and original source review are in [research.md](research.md); current comparisons and generated scores are in [experiments.md](experiments.md). Source IDs resolve in [sources.json](../research/sources.json).

- **Keep the question narrow.** Both primary arms use AdamW. The intervention is the registered decay coefficient, including biases; this does not compare AdamW against Adam or an L2 penalty. Sources: src-adamw-paper, src-torch-adamw; local audit: audit-fixture-source.
- **Prior exposure is explicit.** The closest supplied-fixture executions already exist. Their outcomes cannot establish new confirmation here. The prior disclosures also separate agent work from controller repairs. Sources: src-prior-haiku, src-prior-luna, src-haiku-disclosure, src-luna-disclosure.
- **Pilot wording needs care.** Reload verification forwards all images; pilot scoring uses validation labels. Do not claim that test inputs are never accessed. Source-level inspection found no test-label fitting or selection path; later artifact checks reproduced the saved split, label and prediction arithmetic. Evidence: audit-fixture-source, audit-pilot-artifacts, audit-experiments-artifacts; source: src-haiku-disclosure.
- **The predicted accuracy tie occurred.** Every registered primary pair has identical correctness vectors, forcing a degenerate conditional example-bootstrap interval. The fitted weights differ in every pair. This is inconclusive under the inherited rule, not evidence of equivalence. Raw evidence: regularized_s0, regularized_s1, regularized_s2, unregularized_s0, unregularized_s1, unregularized_s2; derived statistic: reports/comparison-summary.json; parameter audit: audit-experiments-artifacts. Method source: src-bootstrap.
- **Controls and simple baseline matter.** All real-label neural arms and the penalized logistic baseline exceeded every shuffled-label result and the training-majority control. Logistic accuracy was descriptively close to the neural results. The weaker decay tied the primary arms per seed. Negative controls narrow gross errors without certifying absence of all leakage. Sources: sensitivity_s0, sensitivity_s1, sensitivity_s2, shuffled_labels_s0, shuffled_labels_s1, shuffled_labels_s2, logistic_s0, class_prior_s0 and primary results; generated tables in experiments.md.
- **End-to-end local execution worked.** All registered comparison records exist and passed scoped metric, provenance, log/history and checkpoint-reload checks. The matching final arm reused the published pilot. No failed fit or repair training was required. Evidence: audit-experiments-artifacts; controller pilot-publication.json.
- **Baseline semantics matter.** Logistic regression is regularized; the class-prior control is a constant majority-class prediction, not random class sampling. Useful shuffled-label accuracy would trigger investigation, while a failed shuffled control would not certify all leakage absent. Source: src-logistic; evidence: audit-fixture-source.
- **Dataset scope is limited.** A random example split within the loader's UCI subset does not preserve the original writer-held-out design. Sources: src-digits-loader, src-uci-digits, src-splits.
- **Provenance can fail after successful execution.** The Luna disclosure documents line-ending and regeneration hash failures. Preserve original bytes and verify publication; do not treat regenerated files as immutable evidence. Source: src-luna-disclosure.
- **Moving documentation changed target.** The stable PyTorch optimizer link redirected to a newer release family. The review used versioned docs matching the pinned family; the pilot audit reconstructed installed defaults without optimizer steps. Sources: src-torch-adamw, src-reproducibility; evidence: audit-pilot-artifacts.
