# Review of the fixed digits replication

Stage: distillation. Analysis kind: **exploratory**. This is one substantive code, science, citation, reproducibility, security and presentation review. It does not convert the previously exposed study into confirmation or establish general agent reliability.

## Objective and scope

The project goal is verified engineering completion with reproducible artifacts. This phase's immediate objective was to test the documented non-fitting examples, reconcile claims with saved evidence, inspect the paper and website, repair concrete defects, and identify residual limits. Predictions and competing explanations were recorded before checks in [review-plan.md](../research/review-plan.md). Reusing the existing artifact audit and small script probes was more informative per unit time than repeating training: they directly test drift, scoring, packaging and presentation alternatives while preserving the frozen question. No training, tuning, new experiment, model call, collaborator, Git mutation or upload occurred.

## Residual limits and decision

The in-app browser failed to connect because no backend was discovered. The Windows fallback then stopped because it could not determine the current URL confidently enough to enforce its policy. No further UI automation was attempted. **The website was inspected as source and exercised through a minimal DOM/canvas adapter; it was not visually inspected in a browser.** Responsive layout, actual browser canvas rendering, keyboard/screen-reader behavior and deployed-page behavior remain unverified. The paper was visually inspected. No deployment or upload is claimed here.

The website's all-run loss chart has no per-run legend; its experiment filter provides an individual-run view, and the paper/static comparison plots provide labeled trajectories. This is a remaining presentation limitation, not evidence of a different scientific result. Pandoc/pdfLaTeX compiled successfully while emitting a MiKTeX update-check notice, preserved in [the rendering log](logs/review-presentation-final.log). The frozen research environment was not upgraded during review.

Scientific limits remain: prior-result exposure, a fixed example partition without writer IDs, limited seed/coefficient sensitivity, conditional rather than training-population uncertainty, no equivalence test, no calibrated-probability or new-data evaluation, and no independent fresh-environment or cross-platform reproduction. Passing artifact checks cannot eliminate unknown bugs or leakage classes. The result supports only the local accuracy tie and usable saved artifacts, as expressed by the existing exploratory claims.

No substantive missing evidence was found that requires reopening an earlier scientific phase. Continue to the controller's next phase, preserving the presentation repairs on regeneration. Publication and operational status snapshots remain controller-owned. The supplied review draft verification passed with no errors; its exact result is preserved in [review-verification.json](../research/review-verification.json) and does not constitute phase acceptance or scientific validation.
